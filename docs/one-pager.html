RELAY Dispatch Desk

From intake to doorstep: explainable rider recommendations with AI on top, and automatic exception handling.
Next.js 15 · PostgreSQL · Prisma · Auth.js · OpenRouter
Deployed on Vercel + Neon · 100+ automated tests + Playwright e2e

1Problem and solution

Dispatchers juggle orders in spreadsheets and chat. Riders silently ignore offers, jobs get stuck "assigned", capacity is double-booked, and nobody can say why a job is late.

RELAY is one desk where every job has a strict lifecycle, every offer has a deadline, every failure lands in a Manual Queue with a reason, and every action is in an append-only audit trail.

2Dispatcher workflow

Create job→Review ranked riders→Assign / override + reason→Rider confirms→Picked up→Delivered
  • Dashboard answers "what next?": KPIs plus Needs attention (Manual Queue, offers about to expire, deadlines at risk, jobs unassigned for more than 10 min).
  • Job page: recommendations with score breakdown, why other riders were excluded, a live countdown, and the full timeline.
  • Rider app (mobile web): accept or reject offers, mark pickup and delivery, set availability.
  • Idempotent creation (Idempotency-Key) means double submits never create two jobs.

3Recommendations: code decides, AI refines

Hard rules→Score /100→AI re-ranks top 5→Validated or fallback
  • Eligibility: online, serves both zones, enough free capacity, under job limit, has not rejected this job.
  • Score: travel 35 · capacity 20 · workload 20 · deadline slack 15 · reliability 10 (HIGH/URGENT shift weight to speed). Deterministic tie-breaks.
  • OpenRouter LLM predicts acceptance probability and ETA with a one-line reason per rider. Strict JSON schema, 8 s timeout, and an allow-list of eligible ids. Any invalid output falls back to the score ranking.
  • Overrides need a reason and are compared with what the dispatcher actually saw.

4Automatic queue handling

AWAITING_CONFIRMATION→ 2 h (configurable) →MANUAL_QUEUE→Reassign
  • Each offer gets expiresAt. A timeout marks it EXPIRED (history kept), releases capacity, records the reason, and re-opens the job.
  • Lazy expiry on every read and write, plus Vercel Cron as the safety net. The same idempotent sweep (SKIP LOCKED) expires each offer exactly once.
  • A rejection follows the same path. The AI Suggest button proposes reassign, wait, cancel or escalate.
  • A simulated clock and adjustable threshold let reviewers test without waiting 2 hours.

5Architecture and data model

6Key trade-off

One Next.js app on Postgres, with no queues or workers. Row locks, conditional updates and constraints prevent double assignment and overbooking and give exactly-once timeouts. The cost: with no timer at expiresAt, an overdue row flips on the next read, write or cron run. It is never visible as stale, but it is not push-precise.
